# Working in this repo

## Social media

Hunter & Hunter / Jack Hunter social media work does **not** happen here. The
`hunter-social` skill and the card renderer live in the Equity Market repo
(`github.com/jackhunterking/equity-market`), because they render from the
offering seeds — the buildings, their addresses and their photographs — which
moved there with the portal.

If someone asks for a post, a caption, a carousel or a week's batch, work in that
repo instead. Drafting here means drafting without the voice, the investment
arguments, the compliance tier gate, or any way to produce the images.
